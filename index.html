Game

ColoRogue

I have been working for over a year on a hardcore deckbuilder roguelike called ColoRogue. It is played on a 5×5 board of colorful tiles. You align them and interact with them in various ways to meet the exponentially higher target score every round.

It combines themes of color theory, fishing, hell and heaven, and ancient mythology. It is in early access and has rough edges. The main one is the UI, which is plainer than the final version will be. The content so far adds up to about 3,000 hours to 100% all achievements. It is a completely solo project that I made by myself.
