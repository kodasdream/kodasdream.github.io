How to read these numbers

  • Best set is the reps and kg on the Overview page for that lift.
  • Estimated 1RM is the sheet's own figure. Most come from the Epley formula, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), and estimates from sets above about 12 reps tend to run high.
  • Tested means a single: squat, deadlift, triceps pushdown, French bar curl and grip.
  • Bodyweight lifts (dips, push-ups, pull-ups) use the sheet's own estimate, not added weight.
