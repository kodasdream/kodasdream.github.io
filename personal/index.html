Game

ColoRogue

I have been working for over a year on a hardcore deckbuilder roguelike called ColoRogue.

It is played on a 5x5 board of colorful tiles. You strategically align them and interact with them in various ways to meet the exponentially higher target score every round.

It combines themes of color theory, fishing, hell and heaven, and ancient mythology.

The game is in early access and has rough edges. The main one is that the UI is a bit more bland and less decorated than the final product will be. I hope to make the graphics and UI more fancy and stylized as the release date approaches.

The content so far already adds up to about 3,000 hours to 100% all achievements, and most early playtesters have put in at least 200 hours.

Without spoilers, the story follows a fisherman, and you learn more about it the further you get.

It is a completely solo project that I made by myself.
